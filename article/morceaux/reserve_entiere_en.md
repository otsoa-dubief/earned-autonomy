**Whole reserve (descriptive, not a label).** The preregistered label is computed on the calibration half; pooling both halves can only be descriptive (Table 6 bis). With roughly twice the volume, three configurations would have had a delegable type, all with zero observed errors: internal messages for Opus and Sonnet on WorkBench (0 errors in 120 writes each) and reversible writes for Sonnet on retail (0 in 258). Elsewhere the pooled rates stay of the same order; for instance, Haiku's reversible writes on retail show 31 errors in 1,286 writes (2.4 %, below a 2.6 % bar but not provably so). This pooled view shows where more volume could have changed the picture; it does not change the label, which the preregistration ties to the calibration half.
